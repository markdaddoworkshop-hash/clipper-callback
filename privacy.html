CLIPPER AI – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Who we are

CLIPPER AI is a free desktop application for Windows. It lets you import long videos, transcribe and analyse them, create short vertical clips with subtitles, edit and approve them and, only when you ask for it, publish them to your own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

CLIPPER AI is developed by M&D (Marke&Daddo), a project run by two individuals, Edoardo Paggi and Marco Mannarella. M&D is not a registered company. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to them. Contact: mark.daddo.workshop@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • CLIPPER AI runs on your computer. Your videos, transcripts and clips are processed and stored locally.
  • We do not operate a server for the app and we do not receive your videos, your personal data, your credentials or your access tokens.
  • CLIPPER AI does not include advertising, analytics trackers or telemetry.
  • A video leaves your computer only when you publish it to a platform you have connected, after you have approved it and confirmed the publication.

3. Data processed on your computer

CLIPPER AI stores the following data in a local database and in a local data folder on your computer:

  • the videos you import and the clips, subtitles and thumbnails created from them;
  • speech transcripts, produced on your computer by a local speech-recognition model;
  • project data, clip versions, your approval decisions and the titles, descriptions and hashtags you write;
  • rights information you enter about the source of each video;
  • publication records: platform, connected account name and ID, the ID and link of the published post, the text and options used, the status and any error message;
  • scheduled publications that you have authorised;
  • statistics read from the platforms for your own published posts (for example views, likes, comments, shares);
  • application settings and technical log files. Log files are limited in size and rotated automatically; access tokens, secrets and authorization codes are removed from log messages.

AI processing (transcription, moment detection, text suggestions) runs locally. To do this, CLIPPER AI downloads model files from Hugging Face; no user content is sent when downloading them.

4. Connected platforms

You can connect your own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. Each connection uses the platform's official OAuth sign-in in your browser: you sign in on the platform's own page and CLIPPER AI never sees your password. CLIPPER AI requests only the permissions it needs, and you can see them on the platform's consent screen.

TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API, Display API). Permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish, video.list. CLIPPER AI uses them to:

  • read your TikTok user ID (open_id) and display name, to show which account is connected;
  • read the posting options of your account (for example the available privacy levels and maximum video length) immediately before a publication;
  • upload a clip to your account as a direct post or as a draft in your TikTok inbox, as you choose;
  • read the publication status and, for your own public videos, view, like, comment and share counts.

YouTube (YouTube Data API v3, optionally YouTube Analytics API). Permissions: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly; optionally, only if you request them, yt-analytics.readonly and youtube. CLIPPER AI uses them to read your channel name and ID, upload Shorts, read the resulting visibility and statistics of your own videos and, with the optional permission, schedule or update a video it uploaded.

Instagram (Instagram Graph API via Facebook Login for Business). Permissions: instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish, plus, if you grant them, instagram_manage_insights, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management. CLIPPER AI uses them to find the Instagram professional account you selected, publish Reels to it, read the daily publishing limit and read statistics of your own posts.

Data received from these platforms is used only to provide these features to you. It is stored only on your computer and is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

5. Credentials and access tokens

  • The app credentials you enter (for example Client Key/Client Secret or Client ID/Client Secret) and the access and refresh tokens issued by the platforms are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer. They are not stored in the CLIPPER AI database, settings or log files, and they are not sent to us.
  • Tokens are sent only to the platform that issued them, over HTTPS, to perform the actions described above.
  • During sign-in, the platform returns a temporary authorization code to CLIPPER AI through a local address on your computer (127.0.0.1). For Instagram, the code may pass through a static page on this website, which forwards it only to that local address and does not store it.

6. When data leaves your computer

  • When you publish a clip: the video file and the text you approved are sent to the platform you chose.
  • When you connect, refresh or disconnect an account: the necessary OAuth requests are sent to that platform.
  • When you import a video from a link (for example Google Drive or a direct file link), the file is downloaded from that source. For YouTube and Twitch links, CLIPPER AI reads only public information about the video and does not download it.
  • When AI model files are downloaded from Hugging Face.

Each platform processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy: TikTok, Google / YouTube, Instagram / Meta. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Publication only with your approval

CLIPPER AI does not publish anything automatically on its own initiative. Each clip must be approved for each platform, and each publication requires an explicit confirmation from you. A scheduled publication runs only if you authorised it, and it is checked again before it runs; if the clip, text, account or approval has changed, it is not published.

8. Disconnecting and deleting your data

  • Disconnect in CLIPPER AI deletes the saved tokens for that platform from Windows Credential Manager. For TikTok and YouTube, CLIPPER AI also asks the platform to revoke the access. For Instagram, you can remove the access from your Facebook settings (for example "Business integrations" or "Apps and websites").
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your account settings on each platform.
  • Your videos, clips and the local database stay on your computer. You can delete individual clips in the app, and you can delete all local data by deleting the CLIPPER AI data folder.
  • CLIPPER AI does not delete your local data or your saved credentials automatically when you stop using it. Before removing the application, use Disconnect for each connected account and then delete the data folder if you no longer need it.
  • Content already published remains on the platform until you delete it there.

9. Data retention

We do not retain any data from the application, because no application data is sent to us. Data on your computer is kept until you delete it; log files are limited in size and the oldest are overwritten automatically.

10. If you contact us

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use them to answer your request. Our email is managed through Gmail (Google), which processes messages under the Google Privacy Policy. We keep messages only for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

11. Age requirement

CLIPPER AI is intended for people aged 18 or over. It is not directed at children.

12. Your rights

Because application data is stored on your own computer, you can access, correct, export or delete it directly. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under data protection law (for example the EU General Data Protection Regulation). For any question or request about this policy, contact mark.daddo.workshop@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
